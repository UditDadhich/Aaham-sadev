The Name

The Meaning

Every great brand begins with an intention. Ours began with a word — two words, in fact — drawn from the oldest language of introspection, Sanskrit. Together, they form not just a name, but a declaration.

Aham

The Self

In Sanskrit philosophy, Aham is the seat of consciousness — the innermost self that exists before titles, before roles, before the world names you.

Sadev

The Eternal

Sadev speaks of permanence — of things that do not diminish with time, but deepen. A woman's grace. A fabric's story. A craft handed down.

2018

Where It Began

Our Beginning

Aham Sadev was born from a deeply personal belief — that Indian women deserve clothing as considered and complex as they are. Not fast fashion repackaged in ethnic motifs, but garments built on intention: sourced with care, woven with patience, and designed to carry meaning.

We believe in slow fashion. In fewer pieces, made better. In clothing that does not ask you to be a trend — only to be yourself.
